# Use Cases

Civic Sentinel is designed to be flexible. Here are ways communities use it.

## Cities & Municipalities

### Issue Management
- Citizens report potholes, broken lights, flooding
- AI suggests category and priority
- Routes to correct department
- Tracks resolution transparently

## Universities & Campuses

### Safety & Maintenance
- Students report facility issues
- Route to facilities or security
- Anonymous reporting supported

## Neighborhoods

### Community Tracking
- Common area problems
- Maintenance requests
- Transparent status updates

## Environmental Groups

### Monitoring
- Report pollution, dumping
- Photo evidence with location
- Track violations

## Emergency Response

### Disaster Coordination
- Crowdsource damage reports
- Priority mapping
- Coordinate resources

## How to Adapt for Your Community

1. Fork the repository
2. Customize categories
3. Configure notifications
4. Deploy
5. Invite your community
